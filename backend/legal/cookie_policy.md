# Cookie and Data Consent Policy

**Effective Date:** March 28, 2026
**Version:** 1.0

## Cookies

We use **session cookies** for authentication. These are essential for the Platform to function and cannot be disabled while using the service.

**We do not use third-party tracking cookies.** We do not use advertising cookies. We do not use analytics cookies from third-party services.

## Local Storage

We store the following in your browser's local storage:
- **Authentication tokens** (for keeping you logged in between sessions)
- **UI preferences** (such as account mode selection)

This data is stored locally on your device and is cleared when you log out.

## Analytics

We collect **anonymous usage analytics** to improve the Platform. This includes:
- Pages visited and features used (aggregated, not individual)
- Error reports for debugging (automatically deleted after 90 days)
- API performance metrics (automatically deleted after 30 days)

This data is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.

## Your Control

- You can clear all local data by logging out or clearing your browser data.
- You can delete your account and all associated data through Profile settings.
- You can export your data at any time through the Profile > Export Data feature.
