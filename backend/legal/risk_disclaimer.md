## Backtesting Limitations

**The backtesting engine uses historical data with simulated execution. Real market conditions include slippage, liquidity gaps, and execution delays that may significantly differ from simulated results.**

Specific limitations include:
- **Survivorship Bias:** Historical data may not include delisted or failed securities.
- **Look-Ahead Bias:** The system is designed to prevent this, but users should verify.
- **Curve Fitting:** Strategies optimized on historical data may not perform in live markets.
- **Transaction Costs:** Simulated trades may not fully account for brokerage fees, taxes (STT, GST), and stamp duty.
- **Slippage:** Real orders may execute at different prices than simulated fills.
- **Liquidity:** Simulated trades assume infinite liquidity; real markets do not.

## Paper Trading

- Paper trading provides simulated execution to help you test strategies without risking real capital.
- Paper trading results will differ from live trading due to execution differences.
- Paper trading does not account for the psychological impact of real money at risk.
